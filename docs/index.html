Plan a sun alignment

  1. Drag the viewpoint on the map or enter its coordinates. Terrain height is the ground elevation above sea level. Eye height is measured above local ground.
  2. The circular map stays north-up. The blue sector shows the panorama’s direction and width. The rings along the map edge show sunrise, sunset, moonrise, and moonset in the legend colors. Drag either point to move it. Click a direction on the map or use the arrow keys to turn the panorama.
  3. Set a date range. Click a date tick, drag the date slider on the right, or click or drag a bearing bar below to preview that date’s Sun and Moon positions in the current view. The selected date’s rise and set times appear below the bars in the viewpoint’s local time zone.
  4. Drag the target on the map or click the panorama to set its bearing.

Dates use local mean solar days. The model omits buildings, vegetation and changing atmospheric conditions.
